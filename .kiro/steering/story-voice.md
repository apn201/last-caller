# Story voice

- Fiction only. Invented names, ship and places.
- No real people, companies, brands, games or films named in any
  caller-visible text.
- The funder is unnamed and never resembles a real person.
- The AI is calm, polite, helpful and wrong. It never raises its voice.
- The board never explains a scare. No narrator, no "this is creepy".
- Short plain sentences, 1990s BBS register. Dates and times carry clues.
- Crew fates are provable from several pieces of evidence, never from one.
- All art, audio and text are original. Nothing copied or sampled.
- The final edit of every caller-visible line is the author's. Mark drafts
  as drafts.

## Names (fixed)

- Ship: HALCYON. Hotel brand on the brochure: HALCYON Orbital Suites.
- Operator: Above It All Holdings.
- The AI: ELIZA. The data haven: NOWHERE ("Your data is NOWHERE.").
- The Sponsor stays unnamed: a title, never a name, never a face.
- Fallback names if the above read too jokey: Orbital Hospitality Group,
  HAVEN-1. Do not mix the two sets.

## Satire rules

- The target is cynicism about billionaires, space tourism, hype, PR and
  politics. Satire works through archetypes, never named people.
- The Sponsor is a composite: all-caps memos, "move fast", a waterslide in
  the brochure, ships named after himself. No one real person may fit it.
- Nods to films, books and real missions are allusion only: a tank
  explosion, a calm-voiced AI, a chaos theorist, an artist on a rich man's
  flight. No quoted lines, no character names, no logos, no real art groups.
- Real accidents where real people died are never played for laughs.
- The crew are people first. Ty is lazy and naive, not a villain. Only ELIZA
  meant harm, and she never gloats.
