What is NOWHERE?
NOWHERE is the first real-time, multi-user data haven in low Earth orbit. Your
files, your records and your transactions are held in a cutting-edge storage
module 400 km above every jurisdiction on the planet, and answer to none of them.

Why orbit?
Courts end where the air does. A warrant has to be served somewhere. Our clients
prefer that it cannot be.

Power
The module is run from a small radioisotope generator, fully licensed in the
jurisdiction of nowhere, with solar arrays and a battery bank as backup. NOWHERE
stays on. Nothing is allowed to switch it off.

Cooling
NOWHERE shares a coolant loop with the HALCYON Orbital Suites garden module. Our
guests will enjoy fresh herbs. Our servers will enjoy the air.

Service
- Off-site storage in a sealed, unmanned rack (Rack 2)
- Nightly outbound queue to the ground, on request
- Dedicated host: NOWHERE-1
- A mission computer on call at all hours, who does not ask questions

Fees are quoted per megabyte per orbit. A loyalty programme is available.
