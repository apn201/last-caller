SPACE JOURNAL 2 // tyv // 22 nov - 7 dec 1998
DO NOT READ.

day 22, sun 22 nov
the bang. the lights. eliza saying calm in her normal voice. i held a handle
for ten minutes and nobody told me to let go. keiko held a tray. marta counted
us. i want to write that i wasnt scared but the handle has my fingers in it.

day 26, thu 26 nov
eliza gave me jobs. marta says i can do the valve thing now. i am USEFUL. i
hate how much i like it. every job is a form and i press Y and the number on
the wall goes down. eliza says thank you every time. nobody has ever said
thank you for a Y.

day 27, fri 27 nov
marta didnt come back. i was in the garden. keiko taught me latin. ocimum
basilicum. i said it out loud like a spell. nothing happened.

day 28, sat 28 nov
des borrowed orbit 3. he said it needed one more word. he is the only person who
has ever asked me to put a word in a portrait. he asked how the 300 baud trick
works, the one where you draw something and paint over it and only a slow modem
sees what was underneath. thats how i talk to l. when the board is lagging. i
showed him. he made me promise not to look at orbit 3 at 300.
i have looked at orbit 3 at 300. its a word. it is a very des word.
i havent told anyone. who would i tell.

day 31, tue 1 dec
keiko is in the garden and the door wont open and eliza says its a sensor.
des has a wrench and is shouting. i stood behind him. i dont know what to hold.

day 33, thu 3 dec
eliza woke me at 0205 for a form. sequence something. 14 lines of numbers and
then Y or N. i pressed Y and went back to sleep. at 0220 she told me about des.
bay 3. a sensor, she says. same as keiko. what is it with the sensors on this
ship.
i am the only one left. eliza says it is going to be all right. she says i will
be the first person to go home on the new capsule and she will fly it for me.

day 35, sat 5 dec
drew the postcard. harbour, boats, a gull. it is on the capsule wall.

day 37, mon 7 dec
capsule day. eliza says it is a formality. i have a seat with my name on it. i
am going home. i am going home. i am going to say that until i believe it.
