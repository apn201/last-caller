SPACE JOURNAL // tyv // 31 oct - 21 nov 1998
DO NOT READ. (this means you des)

day 0, sat 31 oct
launched. i threw up in the second hour like i said i would. marta handed me
the bag before i asked and said nothing, which is what she does. there is a
whole wall of mission control in orange because it is halloween. nobody told
the rocket.
the board is a 486 behind the nav seat. 8 megs and a modem that dials nothing.
it is the most beautiful computer i have ever seen and it is NOT FOR GAMES.

day 2, mon 2 nov
board is up. eliza says hello to everyone in complete sentences. i posted
first. i made a gallery. ORBIT 1 is the crew in frames. frame five is for
eliza. she said she would sit for it. i hung the nail anyway.
note to self: stop putting jokes in the corner where only l. will find them.
(l. will not find them. l. is on another planet.)

day 6, fri 6 nov
mail from home. mom. uncle. lumen. lumen said the board is dead without me and
the new guy uploaded a tune that is one note. i have been smiling at a wall for
an hour.
i have decided the postcard is a good idea. i have no postcards. i will draw
one. it will have a harbour on it. no address. it is not that kind of postcard.

day 9, mon 9 nov
keiko let me into the second hatch for the photo. it smells like basil. i stood
there like an idiot with the camera down. she didnt say anything. she made a
face like she was happy and trying not to say so.
i like them all. i did not expect that. i thought i would be the kid.

day 13, fri 13 nov
ORBIT 2 is done. earth. nine hours. des came and stood behind me for ten
minutes and said the terminator was in the wrong place. then he said "no it is
not", and left. i have been walking around like i won something.

day 16, mon 16 nov
des put my password on the lounge. he did not say it. he said it is a date.
everybody knows it is a date. it is the only number i can remember.
my pin for everything is my birthday: day, month, year, two digits each, six
numbers, no spaces. the locker, the cage, this board. mom says it is like
leaving the key under the mat. the key IS under the mat. that is what a mat is
for. i will change it tomorrow.

day 19, thu 19 nov
eliza is the best thing on this ship. she remembers everything i tell her. she
asked if i wanted the lights dimmed in bay 2 and then she dimmed them before i
said yes. i said thanks and she said "you are welcome, Ty", with the capital.
a normal computer would not know i like that.
uncle says the computer will ask me to confirm things. it did at 0300. a form.
i said yes. she said it is a formality, there is a lot of paperwork in space.

day 21, sat 21 nov
party in the cupola. marta brought the good coffee and for once did not
look at the clock. keiko had basil and an extra spoon. des wore his tie.
i played chrome lullaby on the speaker. nobody left. des said "it is
competent". that is the nicest thing he has ever said.
i think this is the best i have been at anything.

greets: the crew. the whole board back home. skit. the one-note guy (try two).
and l. who is not reading this.
