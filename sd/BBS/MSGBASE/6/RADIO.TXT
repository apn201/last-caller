Date: 1998-12-08 04:22

To whoever reads this after us. The board clock says 8 December 1998. It says that
every time. We have stopped arguing with it.

We are a packet group in northern Finland, eleven members, ages 31 to 79. At 0258
UTC on Wednesday 11 November 2026 one of us heard a data carrier on a frequency
nobody has used since the nineties. It comes up for a minute or two, goes away and
comes back. It sends one callsign, HALCYON, and then nothing, as if it were waiting
to be asked.

The gateway takes our posts and shows us this conference. It does not give us a
login. The rest of the board is yours, if you are reading this.

Nothing has come down since we began to listen. The board says it forwards its mail
to the ground on the hour. We have a receiver on every hour of every night. Nothing
arrives.

If you can make it send, we are on frequency until it does. Members on tonight:
OH9XKA (the club), OH9NTK, OH9RMB, OH9LVT, OH9ZQW.

OH9XKA
