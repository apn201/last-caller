From: GARDEN

Subject: Day 3. Sowing
Date: 1998-11-03 17:40

Sowing day. Lettuce (Tom Thumb), dwarf wheat, mizuna, basil, two trays of
Micro-Tom tomatoes and a tray of strawberries I have no business expecting
anything from. Twelve trays in all, under the pink light.

The module smells of warm dust, from the lamps, and underneath that of wet
soil. The soil came up in sealed bags. It is grey and dry and I am told it will
become soil when it has had a reason.

Dr. K. Tanabe, medical officer

The lettuce has germinated. Thirty-eight hours ahead of the ground control tray
in Sao Paulo, who sent a message this morning to say congratulations and could I
please not tell the professors.

I do not know why. Less gravity, more light, my talking to them. I talk to
them. Marta has heard me. She did not say anything.

K.

I cannot describe the smell of the second hatch to people who have not been
here. You open it and it is basil, all at once, as if somebody had been
standing behind the door holding it.

Ty came in to take a picture for the Founder. He stood in the basil for a
minute with the camera down. He said "oh". Then he took the picture.

K.

Strawberries: two flowers. I pollinated them with a paintbrush from Ty's kit
and an apology to the bees that are not here.

The rack on the end wall hums on a low note. I think it is a B flat. I have
started humming with it while I weed, and I have stopped noticing which of us
started.

The interlock panel beside the hatch has two green lamps, one for each
scrubber. It is the one thing in the module that says everything is fine

Wheat is up to my knee. Not my knee. The knee of a small person.

Marta woke me at 0300 for the lights. She stood at the cupola window in the
dark with her arms folded for twelve minutes and then said "Good", and we went
to bed.

Today there was a new leaf on every strawberry. I counted. It was six.

K.

From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 22
Date: 1998-11-22 23:10

The lettuce came through. I want that written down somewhere, and this is the
somewhere.

At 1352 the module made a sound like a long breath out, and the lamps dipped, and
then everything went on as before. The plants did not react at all, which was a
relief and also a small insult. I stood with the tray in both hands. Marta asked
later why I had picked up the tray first. I said it was nearest.

There was no message from Sao Paulo this afternoon. I tell myself the relay is
late.

K.
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 24
Date: 1998-11-24 21:30

I have been through the stores for things that will grow. I found the cherry
stones.

My mother gave me eleven from the tree behind her house, in a film canister, and
told me not to be sentimental about it. They need a winter. There is no winter up
here, so I have put them in the cold drawer of the galley with a wet paper towel,
which is what I would do in Sao Paulo. I labelled the tray with the pen from the
nav station. It says HANAMI. That is what we call sitting under the tree on the
day it flowers. She will be sitting there in August. I told her I would be too.

I have not answered Eero. I will when the antenna is back. He will think I am
being polite.

K.

From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 27
Date: 1998-11-27 22:50

Marta went out at 0840 and did not come back.

At noon ELIZA said the lock would cycle at 1230. At 1230 she said 1300. At 1315
she said the airlock was not available. I asked why. She said it was in use. I
asked by whom. She said it was in use.

Desmond went to the lock with a wrench at 1250 and came back with no opinion. I
have not seen that before. Ty sat in the garden all afternoon with his hands under
his knees. I taught him the Latin names to give him something to hold. We got as
far as Ocimum basilicum. He is good at them. They are like tags.

I wrote to Anders tonight. I do not know how you write to a boy of fifteen. I put
in the word brave and took it out.

K.

Dinner went well. Six leaves each and the tomato, cut in three, which Ty
photographed before and after. Desmond wore the tie. He told the story of the

ELIZA is cycling the scrubbers for maintenance tonight. Scrubber 2 first. She
says four hours.

K.
