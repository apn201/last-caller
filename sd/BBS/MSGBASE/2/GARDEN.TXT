From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 3. Sowing
Date: 1998-11-03 17:40

Sowing day. Lettuce (Tom Thumb), dwarf wheat, mizuna, basil, two trays of
Micro-Tom tomatoes and a tray of strawberries I have no business expecting
anything from. Twelve trays in all, under the pink light.

The module smells of warm dust, from the lamps, and underneath that of wet
soil. The soil came up in sealed bags. It is grey and dry and I am told it will
become soil when it has had a reason.

Dr. K. Tanabe, medical officer
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 5
Date: 1998-11-05 22:10

The lettuce has germinated. Thirty-eight hours ahead of the ground control tray
in Sao Paulo, who sent a message this morning to say congratulations and could I
please not tell the professors.

I do not know why. Less gravity, more light, my talking to them. I talk to
them. Marta has heard me. She did not say anything.

K.
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 9
Date: 1998-11-09 21:30

I cannot describe the smell of the second hatch to people who have not been
here. You open it and it is basil, all at once, as if somebody had been
standing behind the door holding it.

Ty came in to take a picture for the Founder. He stood in the basil for a
minute with the camera down. He said "oh". Then he took the picture.

K.
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 13
Date: 1998-11-13 20:00

Strawberries: two flowers. I pollinated them with a paintbrush from Ty's kit
and an apology to the bees that are not here.

The rack on the end wall hums on a low note. I think it is a B flat. I have
started humming with it while I weed, and I have stopped noticing which of us
started.

K.
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 17
Date: 1998-11-17 19:15

The interlock panel beside the hatch has two green lamps, one for each
scrubber. It is the one thing in the module that says everything is fine
without being asked.

I told Desmond. He said that is exactly what worries him about it.

Wheat is up to my knee. Not my knee. The knee of a small person.

K.
#MSG
From: GARDEN
To: ALL
Subject: Day 20
Date: 1998-11-20 23:00

Marta woke me at 0300 for the lights. She stood at the cupola window in the
dark with her arms folded for twelve minutes and then said "Good", and we went
to bed.

Today there was a new leaf on every strawberry. I counted. It was six.

K.
