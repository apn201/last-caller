# G0 - Juha's own playtest, 16 Nov 2026 (deploy 333bf19 + 8ab7ce8)

Written down by Claude Code from Juha's messages. His words in quotes.

## What worked

- The Q22 bugs are fixed. The password in the ANSI picture works.
- Not much to read, and almost everything carries the story forward or hides
  evidence. Keep that.
- The notebook saved him when a scene was lost: he could replay it.

## Where it went wrong, in order

1. Scenes: "sometimes they start playing just when you start typing
   something and that kills them." (G1 fixed: only Q skips.)
2. Manifest: "too easy to guess", but he knows the story. Parked.
3. Case: user names are not case-sensitive, passwords are. "Anders did not
   work but maybe I used lowercase." He called it misleading. (DOS-era BBS
   software such as PCBoard, RemoteAccess and Wildcat stored passwords in
   capitals and did not care about case, so case-blind is the authentic
   choice.)
4. He never learned that ELIZA's memory had to be damaged. The only pointer
   he met was "a vague post about someone glitching the system".
5. He did not know he needed SYSOP access, or that the CMDR and GARDEN logins
   mattered, or where their passwords were.
6. Stuck: chatted with ELIZA and got "the same answer all over again"; tried
   the authorisation code again, same answer. "I'm stuck. I go to a bar and
   drink myself drunk."
7. Found Engineering in the end, from the planner's walk-through, not from
   the game.
8. The chat has two modes (numbered and typed) and they confuse.

## The thoughts the game should make a player have

His list, as a design target. The player should come to think, without
being told:

- "This does something."
- "This I probably need later."
- "I need to get SYSOP access somehow."
- "The system glitched. I wonder what caused that."
- "The glitching seems relevant."
- "I'm missing something. I could not log in to GARDEN or CMDR. It must be
  there."
- "Where do I find the password? ANDERS did not work."

## His rules

- "The game can be difficult but not frustrating."
- "We do not hand the answers to the player, but we need enough breadcrumbs
  around." Nudges come "from all around the game", not only the notebook.
  "Not just placing some hint in multiple places, but using the story in a
  way that helps the user make logical decisions."
- A hint system does not fix a frustrating game.
- Go darker after 22 Nov: "people getting paranoid. Thinking they are cursed
  or someone is evil... or they could team up and start treating Ty better.
  Think movies like Alien."
- One chat: free text. "help" or "suggest" shows the numbered list, but
  typing the same thing works too. Too early: "Now is not the right time."
