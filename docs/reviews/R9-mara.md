## Notes (severity 1 to 3)

1. ADMIN console shows stale state (2). After `SOURCE RTG`, a later ADMIN login still shows "POWER BUS SOLAR DIRECT", "ATTITUDE TUMBLING", "RTG-1 DISCONNECTED". The login splash still says a new caller has no power. The one-off power-up does not look permanent.
2. Keys eaten after logins and scenes (2). The first key after login, or after an ELIZA scene, vanishes without a message. Several times an empty ENTER was needed to get the menu back.
3. Pager reprints text (1). On page 2 of long messages (#17, #96, #108, #111) the pager reprints text already seen; the "1-23/29" footer is confusing.
4. Hint density (2). COMMS.TXT gives query codes, the 254-character limit and the `release QUEUE DEST` syntax; with Achterberg's "I leave the arithmetic", the final chain is clued twice over.
5. ELIZA chat is thin (2). Free text gets "I see." or "I can tell you about the ship". Answers can arrive out of order with the echo.
6. Manifest feedback (1). "Right rows confirmed two at a time" invites guessing among eight options; the options are easy to eliminate.
7. Password checks (1). HANAMI sits on a tray label and is not tied to Tanabe in any text found: the weakest lock.
8. Heavy middle (1). About 100 messages read before the first puzzle moves; no sign of how much is needed.
9. Console clue (1). The ADMIN console has five query commands; after the opening it is a dead end.
10. Real-sounding name (1). The made-up citation "Hanssen" in #111 is close to a real surname.

## What works

- Voices: Tanabe's lettuce and basil, Achterberg's footnotes, Ty's lowercase and bad poetry, Halvorsen's two-line logs, the Founder's all-caps memos.
- Everyday first: thermostat, galley rota, headphones, the pen labelled BASIL. The dread comes from contrast.
- The Eero thread: the porch-light letters are the best writing on the board, never explained.
- Evidence from several places: AUDIT.LOG, Ty's mail and the journal on the 02:05 prompt; the fourteen refusals; Keiko's Garden note and her mail to her mother.
- Diagnostics: "Crew aboard now: 0. I would like to be accurate about that."
- Scenes: the Bay 3 recording, the memory gap.
- Locks: the 300-baud viewer as a lock; the notebook helps.
- Art: cold, consistent ANSI; the crew tag.
